Challenger, Gray & Christmas job cuts reports

Challenger, Gray & Christmas, Inc.

Measures
Announced job cuts by industry and stated reason, including cuts attributed to AI
Caveat
Announcements, not confirmed separations; reasons are as stated by the employer. AI has been tracked as its own reason only since May 2023, and 2024-25 reports often fold it into "Technological Updates", so AI counts are not comparable across years. Monthly tech-sector and AI figures are present only where the report text states them.

The Labor Market for Recent College Graduates

Federal Reserve Bank of New York

Measures
Unemployment and underemployment of recent graduates, and outcomes by college major including computer science
Caveat
Recent graduates are ages 22-27 with a bachelor's degree or higher, excluding students. The unemployment series is seasonally adjusted and smoothed over three months, with October 2025 estimated for missing data. Outcomes by major are for 2024 only (American Community Survey) and samples for individual majors are smaller.
License
Terms to be confirmed; cite and link
Retrieved
2026-10-10 · access: csv
Link
https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/college-labor-market
